## Welcome to Classcard

Classcard is a modern, cloud-based class-management and education-business platform. It replaces spreadsheets and fragmented tools used by academies, studios, schools, tutors, and sports organizations.

Whether you are an **executive evaluating the platform**, an **administrator setting up your branch**, or a **software engineer integrating with our domain APIs**, this documentation provides everything you need.

## Who Uses Classcard?

Our platform natively serves **5 primary user personas**, balancing simplicity for parents with deep operational control for staff:

| Persona | Primary Interface | Key Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Organization Owners** | Admin Portal | Workspace setup, multi-branch control, staff rates, and business analytics. |
| **Operations Staff** | Admin / Front-Desk | Registrations, attendance, package redemptions, invoicing, and lead follow-ups. |
| **Teachers & Coaches** | Instructor View | Viewing schedules, taking attendance, recording skills/grades, and sharing assets. |
| **Guardians & Students** | PWA / Branded Portal | Booking classes, self-check-in, paying invoices, and profile switching. |
| **Leads & Applicants** | Public Booking Page | Exploring offerings, registering for trial classes, and buying packages. |
