# Platform Overview

Welcome to the overview documentation for Classcard. This section covers the platform's architecture, target users, and core structural components.

## 1. Document Purpose

This document provides a clear understanding of Classcard's architecture, core features, user roles, and typical workflows. It serves as a foundational guide for administrators, developers, and team members to understand how the system operates.

---

## 2. What is Classcard?

**Classcard** is a cloud-based class-management and education-business platform designed to replace traditional spreadsheets and disconnected tools used by:

- Sports academies and swim schools
- Dance and yoga studios
- Music and martial-arts schools
- Tutoring and language centers

The platform combines scheduling, student enrollment, billing, attendance tracking, and communication into a single, unified system.

---

## 3. Users and Roles
Classcard accommodates different types of users with specific permissions:

- **Organization Owner / Administrator:** Configures the organization, branches, staff access, billing rules, and global settings.
- **Front-Office Staff:** Manages daily operations, student records, class rosters, invoices, and payments.
- **Teachers and Coaches:** View schedules, manage assigned classes, mark attendance, and record student progress.
- **Students and Parents / Guardians:** Use the student portal or app to view schedules, book or cancel classes, pay invoices, and track learning progress.
- **Leads / Prospective Students:** Potential students who interact via forms, trial classes, and CRM pipelines before enrollment.

---

## 4. Core Product Model
A standard Classcard organization is built upon these fundamental objects:

1. **Organization / Workspace:** The primary operating environment.
2. **Branch:** A linked workspace for different physical business locations.
3. **Location / Facility:** Physical rooms or courts used for classes.
4. **Product / Package:** Sellable services, memberships, or bundles of sessions.
5. **Class / Event:** Scheduled group or one-on-one educational sessions.
6. **Student / Lead:** Customer and prospect profiles tracking participation and history.
7. **Invoice / Payment:** Financial records connected to purchases and attendance.
