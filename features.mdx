# Core Features

Welcome to the features guide. Here you can see what **Classcard** can do to help manage schools and education centers easily.

---

## 1. Setup and Organization
- **Organization & Branches:** Create your main school and add different branches or locations.
- **Settings:** Set your time zone, currency, working hours, and holidays.
- **Staff Roles:** Give different permissions to owners, managers, and teachers.

---

## 2. Products and Packages
- **Create Services:** Add subjects, classes, and items that you want to sell.
- **Packages & Memberships:** Sell a bundle of classes (for example, 10 swimming sessions) to students.
- **Pricing:** Set prices, taxes, and special discounts easily.

---

## 3. Scheduling and Calendar
- **Easy Calendar:** Schedule group classes or one-on-one lessons on a calendar.
- **Repeating Events:** Make classes repeat daily, weekly, or monthly automatically.
- **Room Assignment:** Assign teachers and physical rooms to each class.

---

## 4. Online Booking
- **Public Booking Page:** Give students a nice web page to book classes online.
- **Online Payment:** Let students pay for packages using credit cards or online payment gateways.
- **Automatic Updates:** The system updates class spaces and waiting lists automatically.

---

## 5. Student Management
- **Student Profiles:** Keep all student info, contact numbers, and notes in one place.
- **Guardians:** Link parents to students so they can manage everything together.
- **Forms:** Collect student information using custom online forms.

---

## 6. Attendance Tracking
- **Mark Attendance:** Teachers can check who is present or absent in every class.
- **Self Check-in:** Students can check in by themselves.
- **Session Count:** The system automatically reduces used sessions from the student's package.

---

## 7. Billing and Payments
- **Invoices:** Create and send bills to students automatically.
- **Payment Records:** Track who paid, who owes money, and give receipts.
- **Reminders:** Send automatic payment reminders before due dates.

---

## 8. Communication
- **Reminders:** Send automatic email or text messages so students do not miss classes.
- **Announcements:** Send messages to groups of students or staff quickly.

---

## 9. Mobile Apps
- **Staff App:** Teachers can check schedules and mark attendance on their phones.
- **Student App:** Students and parents can view schedules, book classes, and pay bills easily.
